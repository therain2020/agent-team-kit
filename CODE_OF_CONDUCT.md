# Code of Conduct

This project follows the [Contributor Covenant, version 2.1](https://www.contributor-covenant.org/version/2/1/code_of_conduct/).

## Our pledge

We want participation to be harassment-free for everyone, regardless of age, body
size, visible or invisible disability, ethnicity, sex characteristics, gender
identity and expression, level of experience, education, socio-economic status,
nationality, personal appearance, race, caste, color, religion, or sexual identity
and orientation.

## Expected behavior

- Be direct about the work and kind about the person.
- Assume good intent; ask before escalating.
- Give and accept specific, actionable feedback.
- Respect privacy: do not share another person's data, keys or private messages.
- Do not use the project to build or distribute tools for harassment, surveillance
  or unauthorized access.

## Unacceptable behavior

Harassment, discriminatory language, personal attacks, doxxing, sexualized
attention, sustained disruption, and publishing others' private information
without permission.

## Reporting

Report concerns privately to the maintainers. Use the repository's **Security**
tab → **Report a vulnerability** (it is private) or contact a maintainer directly
on GitHub. Do not open a public issue about a conduct incident.

Maintainers will review reports in good faith, keep the reporter's identity
confidential where possible, and may warn, remove content, or ban participation.
For severe incidents, they may act immediately.
